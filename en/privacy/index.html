Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: this site doesn't track you, collects only what you write to it, and shares it with no one. The full version is below — it reads in two minutes.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies, no trackers, no analytics.
  • No user accounts, no database.
  • No mailing list: the checklist downloads freely, no email required.
  • No third-party resources (fonts and files are served by the site itself).

The contact form

The form delivers your message to Aegiren's business mailbox, contact@aegiren.com, hosted on Google Workspace. No other provider is involved: the service that carries the form and the mailbox that receives it belong to the same host — the mail host. What's transmitted is exactly what you choose to write: your email address, your situation, and the optional fields if you answer them.

That information serves one purpose: replying to you. It is never sold, shared, or used for automated prospecting. If delivery fails, the site falls back to your own email application — in which case nothing passes through a third-party service at all.

The 15-control test

The online test runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are never sent, stored or retained: the score and priorities are computed on your device, and everything disappears when you close the page. No email address is required to see the result.

Retention

The exchange is kept as ordinary email correspondence for the duration of the relationship, then deleted. You can request deletion at any time.

Hosting

This is a static site with no dynamic content. Like any host, the provider serving it may log technical connection data (IP address, pages requested) for security and operations; Aegiren adds no tracking on top.

Your rights

Access, rectification, deletion: write to contact@aegiren.com. Reply within 48 business hours.

Publisher

This site is published under the Aegiren brand. Full legal notices will be published when the service opens commercially. No transaction or payment takes place on this site, and nothing is collected beyond what you write to us.
