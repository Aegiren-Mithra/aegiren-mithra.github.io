Five services. A written scope, a deliverable, a timeline.

Three services form the core of the approach — audit, harden, document — and two complementary ones cover what an audit alone can't fix: the human reflex, and the ability to come back after an incident. Every engagement starts with a free call and a written scope. What's not included is said before we start — that, too, is a clean deliverable.

The complementary services

What an audit alone can't fix.

A flawless configuration won't stop an employee handing over their password, nor ransomware encrypting synced files. These two services work over time: the human reflex on one side, the ability to come back on the other. They follow a first review — never replace it.

04 TRAINED

Awareness & simulated phishing

Phishing remains the first way in, because it doesn't attack the machine — it attacks the person. The reflex to pause, verify and report can't be bought as a licence: it has to be maintained.

Scope
  • ✓Ethical simulated phishing campaigns, scenarios matched to your sector
  • ✓Two-to-three-minute micro-trainings triggered for those who click
  • ✓Quarterly report a non-technical leader can read, plus a 12-month click-rate curve
Not included — on purpose
  • ✕No campaign without written authorisation and prior staff information
  • ✕No individual trap, no sanction: we measure the group
